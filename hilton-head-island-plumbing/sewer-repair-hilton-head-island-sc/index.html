    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Hilton Head Island, SC

    When repairs are no longer sufficient due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer various replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive option for your South Carolina property, ensuring a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Hilton Head Island

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Hilton Head Island

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older South Carolina homes.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Hilton Head Island

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone often saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Hilton Head Island

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Hilton Head Island dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Hilton Head Island

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Hilton Head Island, SC

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can shift buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
