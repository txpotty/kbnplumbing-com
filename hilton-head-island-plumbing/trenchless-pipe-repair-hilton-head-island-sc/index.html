    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Hilton Head Island

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Hilton Head Island.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. KBN Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Hilton Head Island, SC.
