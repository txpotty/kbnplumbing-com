    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Hilton Head Island, SC

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Hilton Head Island

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. High humidity accelerates corrosion on exposed copper and fittings in crawlspaces, so a quick look under the house is worth doing every year. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.
