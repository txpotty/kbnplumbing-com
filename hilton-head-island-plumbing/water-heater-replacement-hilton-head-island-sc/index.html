    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Hilton Head Island

    Electric water heaters in Hilton Head Island usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Hilton Head Island

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Parts are easy to get.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Hilton Head Island

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Hilton Head Island

    Much of the housing in South Carolina was built after 1970, so copper, CPVC and PEX supply lines are far more common than old galvanized pipe. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Hilton Head Island

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We lay out the options.
