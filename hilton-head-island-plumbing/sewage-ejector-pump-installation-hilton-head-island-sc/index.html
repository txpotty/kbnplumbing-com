    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Hilton Head Island

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Hilton Head Island, SC

    A dead ejector pump means the basement fixtures stop working until it is fixed. Stop using those fixtures, including the washer if it drains to the pit, then call (864) 588-3813 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer.     Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.
