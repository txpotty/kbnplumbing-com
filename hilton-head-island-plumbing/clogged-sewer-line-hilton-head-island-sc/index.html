    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Hilton Head Island

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix.     Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Hilton Head Island, SC

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks.     Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Hilton Head Island
