    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Hilton Head Island

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Hilton Head Island

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Hilton Head Island

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff.     Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Hilton Head Island

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.
