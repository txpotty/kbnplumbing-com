    Preventive Care and Longevity in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed.     Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Hilton Head Island

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
