    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Hilton Head Island

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Hilton Head Island, SC

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Hilton Head Island

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Hilton Head Island, SC

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
