    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Charleston, SC

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Charleston, SC homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We help you compare.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Charleston

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Charleston, SC

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Leaks only get worse.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Charleston

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Supply houses stock them.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Charleston

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. Registration matters.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Charleston, SC

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Charleston

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Bigger is not always needed.

You see it all up front.

    Being close by lets us move fast when a tank fails. A heater leaking into the garage at night, a pilot that will not stay lit or an electric tank with no hot water after a storm are calls we can often reach the same day, and many are finished before dinner. Trucks carry common tanks.
