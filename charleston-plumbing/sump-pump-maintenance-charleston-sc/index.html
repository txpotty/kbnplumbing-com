    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Charleston, SC

    Routine maintenance usually costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you honestly whether yours has more years left or is ready to retire.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Charleston, SC

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Charleston, SC

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, KBN Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Charleston, SC. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Charleston, SC

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and unheated garages are the ones most likely to burst during a South Carolina cold snap, since many homes were built with little insulation around them.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Charleston, SC

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Charleston

    KBN Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, confirming the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for quick repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Charleston

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Charleston

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.
