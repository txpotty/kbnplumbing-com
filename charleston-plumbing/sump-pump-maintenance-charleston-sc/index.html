    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Charleston

    Hurricanes and tropical storms bring heavy rain to South Carolina, and flooded streets can push water back up through floor drains and low sewer connections. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Charleston, SC

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. With regular service, seven to ten years is typical. We will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is ready to retire.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Charleston, SC

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Charleston, SC

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Hard freezes are not common in South Carolina, but a few nights each winter can drop well below freezing, especially in the upstate.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Charleston

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Charleston

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Charleston

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Charleston, SC

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Sump Pump Maintenance Near Me With Prompt Visits in Charleston, SC

    Local plumbers get there faster, and with a sump pump that matters, because a pit can overflow in under an hour once the pump quits during a storm. We book most pre-storm checks within a few days. Pumps that have stopped during a storm go to the front of the line.

    Water pools in predictable places around a house: the downspout corners, the low side of the yard and wherever the grade slopes back toward the wall. We look at all of that while we are outside checking the discharge. A little regrading can cut the pump's workload.
