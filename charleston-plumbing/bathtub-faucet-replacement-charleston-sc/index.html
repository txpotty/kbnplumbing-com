    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Charleston

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Charleston

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Charleston, SC

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update.
