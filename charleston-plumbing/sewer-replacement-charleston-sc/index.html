    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Charleston, SC

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Charleston, SC

    A sudden sewer line failure can be a major emergency, leading to sewage backups and serious health hazards. KBN Plumbing offers emergency sewer replacement services to address critical issues promptly. Our team is ready to respond quickly, diagnose the problem, and implement a quick, lasting replacement to restore your home's functionality and safety in Charleston.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Charleston

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Charleston

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Charleston, SC

    Many older homes in Charleston, SC still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Charleston, SC

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Charleston.
