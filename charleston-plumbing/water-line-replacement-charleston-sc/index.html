    PEX Water Line Replacement in Charleston

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Charleston, SC

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Hard freezes are not common in South Carolina, but a few nights each winter can drop well below freezing, especially in the upstate. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone.
