    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Work in Charleston, SC

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Charleston, SC

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Charleston

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Charleston, SC

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Charleston, SC

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Charleston

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Charleston, SC

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Charleston, SC

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Charleston

    Sump pump installation in Charleston, SC runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes.     Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. Heavy use shortens that. We can check yours.
