    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Charleston

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Charleston, SC usually ranges from $200 to $600. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    To help it last, avoid putting fibrous or hard items like corn husks, bones, or coffee grounds down the drain. Always run cold water before and after use, and now and then grind citrus peels to keep it fresh. Regular cold water flushing helps maintain the unit.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.
