    Shower Valve Replacement in Charleston

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Charleston, SC

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Charleston, SC

    Many homes in Charleston feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still subject to wear and tear. Our plumbers are very experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specialized knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Charleston, SC

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Charleston

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to allow a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Charleston

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Charleston, SC

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.
