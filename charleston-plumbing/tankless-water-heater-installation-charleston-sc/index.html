    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Charleston, SC

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Private wells are common outside town limits, and some South Carolina well water carries iron, low pH or sediment that can stain fixtures and wear down water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Charleston, SC

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan make up for part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas.     Tank to Tankless Conversions in Charleston

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.
