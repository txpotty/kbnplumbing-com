    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Charleston, SC

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Charleston, SC.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Charleston, SC

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Charleston, SC

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Even with softer supply water in Charleston, a yearly water heater flush still helps clear the sediment that settles in the bottom of the tank. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Charleston, SC, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Charleston

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Charleston.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Charleston

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We service 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Charleston, SC

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Charleston

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.
