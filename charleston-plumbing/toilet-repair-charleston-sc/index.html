    Running Toilet Repair in Charleston

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to significantly higher water bills.     Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Charleston, SC

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are crucial for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Charleston, SC

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit.     Clogged Toilet Clearing in Charleston, SC

    Tough toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt.     Leaking Toilet Repair in Charleston

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. We carefully inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.
