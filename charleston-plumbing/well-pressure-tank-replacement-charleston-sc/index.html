    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Charleston, SC

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank.     Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Charleston, SC

KBN Plumbing expertly replaces these bladder-style tanks across Charleston, SC, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Charleston

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the usable water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the most effective ways to extend pump life.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Charleston

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a firm price before starting.

    Common signs include your well pump repeatedly turning on and off (short cycling), fluctuating water pressure, air sputtering from faucets, or a tank that is waterlogged (feels heavy when tapped and full of water). If you notice any of these, it is time for an inspection.

    Rapid pump cycling, pressure that swings up and down, and water coming out of the air valve are common signs. A tank that sounds solid and full when tapped near the top is often waterlogged.

    KBN Plumbing offers a solid warranty on both the well pressure tank itself (as per manufacturer's warranty) and our installation labor. We stand behind our work to ensure your full satisfaction and peace of mind.

    Regular maintenance for a well pressure tank involves checking the air pressure in the tank at least once a year, ensuring it's properly pre-charged.
