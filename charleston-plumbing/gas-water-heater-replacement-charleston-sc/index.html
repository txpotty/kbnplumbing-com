    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Charleston, SC Home

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charleston

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Charleston

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Charleston

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of South Carolina plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    No Hot Water Calls in Charleston

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Charleston

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Charleston

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Charleston, SC

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.
