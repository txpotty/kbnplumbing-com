    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Charleston, SC

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Charleston

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Red clay dominates much of the upstate, while the coastal plain of South Carolina has sandy soil and a high water table that affects drainage and digging. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak without breaking up the floor to look, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before any concrete gets cut.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Charleston, SC

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Pool Leak Detection in Charleston

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Charleston

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.
