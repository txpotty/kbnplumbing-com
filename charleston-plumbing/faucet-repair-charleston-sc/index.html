    Leaky Faucet Repair in Charleston

    A dripping faucet is more than just an annoyance, it's a waste of water and money. Fixing a leak promptly is one of the simplest ways to conserve water at home. We carry a wide range of common parts so most leaky faucet repairs can be completed in a single visit, restoring your peace and quiet.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Charleston, SC

    Beyond standard kitchen and bathroom fixtures, we handle repairs for specialty faucets such as pot fillers, utility sink faucets, and outdoor spigots found on many homes.     Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Charleston

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Charleston

    A dripping spout usually means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Charleston

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Before a forecast freeze, disconnecting garden hoses, covering hose bibs and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to protect a home. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when needed. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long season of yard use.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Charleston

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, restoring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Charleston, SC

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it difficult to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer expert repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Charleston, SC

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.
