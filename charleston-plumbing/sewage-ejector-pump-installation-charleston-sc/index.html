    Grinder Pump Installation in Charleston

    Where wastewater contains tougher solids, a grinder pump installation in Charleston might be necessary. Unlike standard ejector pumps, grinder pumps macerate waste into a fine slurry before pumping it to the sewer line. KBN Plumbing can help you determine if a grinder pump is the right solution, handling the whole installation from start to finish, including the specialized electrical and discharge piping.
