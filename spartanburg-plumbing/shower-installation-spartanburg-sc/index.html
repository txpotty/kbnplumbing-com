    Steam Shower Plumbing in Spartanburg

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Spartanburg

    KBN Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We make sure of proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is right for your new showerhead.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Spartanburg

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team ensures all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, improving your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more efficient models.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Spartanburg, SC

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.
