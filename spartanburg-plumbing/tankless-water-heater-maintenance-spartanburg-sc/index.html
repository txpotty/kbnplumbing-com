    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Spartanburg, SC

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Spartanburg

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Spartanburg, SC

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. So we schedule the work before opening or after close.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Spartanburg, SC

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Spartanburg

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Spartanburg

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Spartanburg

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.
