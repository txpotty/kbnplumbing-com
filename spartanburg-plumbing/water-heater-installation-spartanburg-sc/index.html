    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them very efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Spartanburg

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings.     Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are increasingly popular in South Carolina homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these efficient units, ensuring proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Spartanburg. We'll help you choose the right model for your particular needs.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.
