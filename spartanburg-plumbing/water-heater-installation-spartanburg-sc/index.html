    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Spartanburg

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.
