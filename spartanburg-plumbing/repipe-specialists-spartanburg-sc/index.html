    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Spartanburg

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Spartanburg. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Spartanburg, SC

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Spartanburg, SC. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Spartanburg

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    A documented repipe can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Buyers and inspectors notice it.

    While some minor wall and ceiling access is usually necessary for a repipe, our repipe specialists use techniques that limit damage.     We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices for repiping in South Carolina, each with distinct advantages. Copper is very durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    Repeated slab leaks, pinhole leaks, low pressure, discolored water or polybutylene, Kitec or galvanized pipe are all signs. Frequent leak repairs are the clearest signal.
