    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Spartanburg

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Spartanburg

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Spartanburg homes.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Spartanburg
