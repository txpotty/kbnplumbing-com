    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Spartanburg

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Spartanburg, SC

    Big open floor plans hide leaks well. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, which lets us rule out large areas before acoustic work starts. It saves an hour on large homes across Spartanburg, SC.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Spartanburg, SC

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Spartanburg

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, so leaks can move more water and be harder to hear from the surface with a standard ground microphone. Our approach changes for those. Our techs combine zone isolation, line tracing and tracer gas, and schedule testing around your operating hours in Spartanburg.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Spartanburg, SC

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Spartanburg, SC

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile cannot wait until next week. Shut off the main. KBN Plumbing sends a tech to Spartanburg, SC homes quickly, isolating the leak so you have water elsewhere while we plan the repair.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Spartanburg, SC

    Older homes in Spartanburg, SC often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. That knowledge points the search at the likeliest spots first.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Spartanburg

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.
