    Drain Field Repair in Spartanburg, SC

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom.     Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Spartanburg

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Spartanburg

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Spartanburg, SC

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age.     House-to-Tank Line Repair in Spartanburg
