    Hydro-Jetting Pre-Inspection in Spartanburg, SC

    Jetting a cracked or collapsing pipe can make it worse. So we look first. A quick scope in Spartanburg, SC shows whether the pipe walls are solid enough to handle thousands of psi or whether a gentler cleaning and a repair plan make more sense. It takes about 20 minutes.
