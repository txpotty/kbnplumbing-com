    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Spartanburg, SC

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Spartanburg

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. KBN Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Spartanburg.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Spartanburg, SC

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Spartanburg

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Spartanburg
