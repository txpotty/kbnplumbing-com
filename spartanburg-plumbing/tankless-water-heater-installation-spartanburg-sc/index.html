    What Goes Into a Tankless Conversion in Spartanburg, SC

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Gas tankless units in Spartanburg are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Spartanburg

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. A far bath may need recirc.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Spartanburg, SC

    Businesses in Spartanburg, SC with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. Peak demand sets the rack size. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Spartanburg

    Conversions in Spartanburg tend to run a full workday. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, the gas line is upsized and tested, a new vent goes out through a wall or roof, and the unit is mounted, plumbed with isolation valves and fired. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Commercial hot water loads in Spartanburg are heavier and more constant than residential. A busy restaurant may run a dish machine, three hand sinks and a mop sink at once, and the tankless rack has to hold temperature through the whole lunch rush without dropping a degree. We rack units with a common manifold. Service one, keep the others going.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg, SC

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Spartanburg, SC homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spartanburg

    Propane homes in Spartanburg can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.
