    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Rock Hill

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Rock Hill

    Wrong temperature means a part is off.     Anode Rod Checks in Rock Hill

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.
