    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Rock Hill is an emergency for a family of five. We take no-hot-water calls first, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. If a heat exchanger is leaking, we shut the water off at the service valves and order the part the same day. Control boards take longer.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Rock Hill

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Resetting the code without a flush only buys a few days, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Rock Hill

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. We check the vent terminal for bird nests and dryer lint, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. A code 10 can also come from a vent run that is too long for the model. We measure.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill

    Restaurants and laundromats in Rock Hill often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. When one unit drops out, the rest carry the load and scale up sooner, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Rock Hill

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.
