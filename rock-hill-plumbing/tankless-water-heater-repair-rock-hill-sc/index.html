    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Rock Hill

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill

    Navien units are known for their advanced condensing technology but can sometimes experience issues with their circulation pump or exhaust systems. KBN Plumbing provides dedicated Navien tankless water heater repair in Rock Hill, addressing common problems like ignition failures or condensate drain blockages.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Hill

    Gas tankless water heaters require correct gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve failures to carbon monoxide sensor errors. Our certified technicians in Rock Hill perform thorough diagnostics on gas lines, igniters and exhaust systems, so your unit is both functional and safe.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Rock Hill

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.
