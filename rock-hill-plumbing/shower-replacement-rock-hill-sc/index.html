    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Rock Hill

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. A new pan is usually $1,500 to $4,000, while a full tiled rebuild can reach $8,000 to $20,000. Valves are the cheapest fix.

    Trim and showerheads are DIY-friendly. Hidden work like valves and pans is best left to a licensed plumber.

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out and a new pan, walls and fixtures go in, with the drain moved as needed. Accessibility improves a lot.

    Replacement keeps the footprint. A replacement stays in place, and a remodel can move walls and change the layout. It is the smaller job.

    If no one uses the tub, a walk-in shower is often safer and more convenient. Keep at least one tub in the home if resale to families matters to you.

    Leaks that come back after repairs are the clearest sign. Cracked pans, returning mold, soft floors and chronic drain clogs all suggest it is time. Looks count too.

    Cosmetic updates are fine to DIY. A full shower replacement involves plumbing, waterproofing and sometimes structural repair, and a mistake in any of them can lead to leaks and damage that cost far more than the job. Hire a licensed plumber.
