    Supply Line Leak Repair in Rock Hill

    Wall Leak Solutions in Rock Hill, SC

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Rock Hill

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.

    Wall Crack Sealing for Basements in Rock Hill

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy adds strength. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Rock Hill, SC

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall finds the weakest point, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor.
