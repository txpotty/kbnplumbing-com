    How Pipe Bursting Works in Rock Hill

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Rock Hill

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.
