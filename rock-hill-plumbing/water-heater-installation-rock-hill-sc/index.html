    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in South Carolina homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these highly efficient units, ensuring proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Rock Hill. We'll help you choose the right model for your particular needs.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Rock Hill

    We are experienced in installing all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements and warranty terms for each manufacturer, making sure your new unit is set up precisely to factory specifications for optimal performance and longevity in your Rock Hill home.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Rock Hill

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Rock Hill

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing a lot with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a safer spot.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Gas water heaters are a popular choice in Rock Hill due to their efficiency and faster recovery rates. Our certified plumbers meticulously install gas lines, ensure secure connections and verify proper ventilation to prevent carbon monoxide buildup. We also install required expansion tanks and check for gas leaks, closely following all local safety codes.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Rock Hill, SC

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting. Attic installs, relocations and code upgrades may add to the price. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, labor, permits and haul-away. We also compare long-term energy costs so you can make an educated choice.

KBN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours.
