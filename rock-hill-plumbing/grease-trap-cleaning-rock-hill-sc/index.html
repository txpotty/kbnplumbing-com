    Why Choose KBN Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Rock Hill, SC?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. Then comes the smell. A rancid, sour odor means the grease cap has started to break down and give off hydrogen sulfide. Hydrogen sulfide also eats concrete, so a neglected interceptor wears out years early.

    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from leaving the trap and hardening in the lateral.
    • Prevent Blockages, Regular pumping keeps floor drains flowing during the dinner rush.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means less corrosion on concrete and baffles.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Rock Hill, SC

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Rock Hill

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.     Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Rock Hill

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. Cracked baffles and missing outlet tees are the usual finds. Small fixes can often happen the same visit.
