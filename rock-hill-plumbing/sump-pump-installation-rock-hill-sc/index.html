    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Rock Hill

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners.     New Sump Pit Installation in Rock Hill

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Rock Hill, SC.
