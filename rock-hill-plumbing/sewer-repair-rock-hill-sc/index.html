    Root Intrusion Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, so we check the vent any time the drains act up but the main line looks clean. We clear it from the roof or replace the damaged section.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Rock Hill, SC

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Rock Hill yards make this a common call.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Rock Hill, SC

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Rock Hill

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.
