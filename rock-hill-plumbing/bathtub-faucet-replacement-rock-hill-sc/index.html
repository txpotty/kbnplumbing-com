    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Rock Hill

    Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run fairly soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts.     Budget Tub Faucet Options in Rock Hill, SC

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. That option comes up first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Rock Hill, SC

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Rock Hill

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Rock Hill, SC

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Rock Hill, SC

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.
