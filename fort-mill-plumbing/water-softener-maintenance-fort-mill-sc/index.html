    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Fort Mill

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Fort Mill, SC
