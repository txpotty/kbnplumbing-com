    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Fort Mill, SC

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Fort Mill

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Fort Mill

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Fort Mill

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Fort Mill, SC

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Fort Mill

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.
