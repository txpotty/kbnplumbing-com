    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Fort Mill

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. KBN Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Fort Mill. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Fort Mill

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so toilets, showers and sinks drain into a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We excavate the floor, set a basin typically 30 to 50 gallons and connect each fixture drain. A solids-handling pump lifts waste up and into the main drain line. The basin lid is sealed and vented to keep odors out.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Fort Mill

    Where wastewater contains tougher solids, a grinder pump installation in Fort Mill might be necessary. Unlike standard ejector pumps, grinder pumps macerate waste into a fine slurry before pumping it to the sewer line. This is often required for certain commercial applications or residential setups with specific waste disposal needs. KBN Plumbing can help you determine if a grinder pump is the right solution, handling the entire installation from start to finish, including the specialized electrical and discharge piping.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Fort Mill, SC

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer line. A smaller ejector or laundry tray pump can handle washer discharge and sink drainage. We install a lint filter where needed to protect the pump. These systems are compact and can often be installed without major floor demolition.
