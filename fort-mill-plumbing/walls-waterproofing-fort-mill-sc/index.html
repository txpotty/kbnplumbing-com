    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Fort Mill

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system typically runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing generally ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains. Don't ignore these indicators, as they often point to ongoing water intrusion.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Fort Mill may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. KBN Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Walls waterproofing is a key step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also mean water is getting behind.

    A shower wall waterproofing project typically takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.
