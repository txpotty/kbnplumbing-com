    Pressure Switch Replacement in Fort Mill, SC

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Fort Mill, SC

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Fort Mill, SC

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Fort Mill, SC

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.
