    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Aiken, SC

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Aiken

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Warm weather for most of the year means water heaters, irrigation lines and outdoor fixtures in South Carolina see steady use with little seasonal rest. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Aiken

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Aiken

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Aiken, SC

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. Exterior block sealing depends on wall length. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Drainage System Integration in Aiken, SC

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Aiken, SC

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Aiken

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Aiken, SC

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Aiken

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system usually runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, but not counting the new tile. Block wall sealing outside often costs $800 to $3,000. Below-grade foundation work generally ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    Every install gets a written warranty on materials and workmanship. Your proposal lists exactly what is covered and for how long, and when a manufacturer offers its own warranty on a membrane or resin we register it in your name. Store the paperwork with your home documents.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Poured concrete takes crystalline coatings well, while block often needs an elastomeric membrane or a drainage board.

    Anywhere from an afternoon to over a week. Interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while exterior excavation with new footing drains might run several days or longer than a week depending on the size of the house. Wet weather can push exterior work back.

    Simple crack injection usually does not need one. Excavation, structural repairs and changes to drainage or the sump discharge often do, and the local building department sets the rules, so we check before we quote and file the paperwork if one is needed. We list any permit fee on the quote.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. We follow each maker's steps exactly.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years. The range is wide since soil movement, groundwater levels and how well it was installed all play a part, and a crack that opens later can let water around even a good membrane. Over time, drain tile can fill with silt.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out.
