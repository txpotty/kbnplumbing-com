    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Aiken

    Many older homes in Aiken, SC still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust and reduced water pressure over time. KBN Plumbing focuses on replacing these outdated pipes with modern, more durable materials like copper or PEX. This upgrade eliminates recurring leaks and rust-colored water and greatly improves water flow and quality throughout your home. We handle the entire process, from initial assessment to complete repiping, with minimal disruption.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Aiken

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is open.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Aiken

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Aiken, SC

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your plumbing.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Aiken, SC

    A single pipe repair is much less expensive than a whole-house repipe and makes sense when the rest of the system is in good shape. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can soon exceed repiping. We look at the age and condition of your plumbing and give you honest numbers for both paths. That way you can choose based on facts rather than pressure.

We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough inspection.

    If the leak is isolated and the rest of the plumbing is in good condition, a repair makes sense. If you have repeated leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene, repiping is usually the better long-term value.

    Our skilled plumbers are equipped to repair all common types of residential and commercial piping found in Aiken, SC homes and businesses. This includes copper, PEX, PVC, CPVC and galvanized steel pipes. We also handle cast iron and lead service lines where applicable.

    Pinhole leaks typically result from water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure or electrical grounding issues that corrode the pipe from the inside.     We can repair polybutylene in an emergency, but because the material is prone to sudden failure, we generally recommend replacing it with PEX or copper. We will give you options for both.

    Freezing temperatures are a common cause of burst pipes, especially when uninsulated pipes are exposed to sustained cold.
