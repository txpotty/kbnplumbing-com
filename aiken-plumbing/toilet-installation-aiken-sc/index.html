    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Aiken

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Aiken, SC

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Aiken

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Aiken

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Aiken, SC

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Aiken, SC

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Aiken, SC

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.
