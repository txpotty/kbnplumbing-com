    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Aiken

    The cost of water softener replacement in Aiken, SC can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any needed plumbing changes. We provide clear, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is better for soap and fixtures.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    New water softeners typically come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from KBN Plumbing. We back our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not usually required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no major changes to the existing plumbing lines.
