    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Aiken

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KBN Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Aiken home.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Aiken

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation.     Softener Loop Installation in Aiken
