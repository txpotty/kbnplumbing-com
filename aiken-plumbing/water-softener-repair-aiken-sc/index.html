    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Repair in Aiken

    The cost of water softener repair in Aiken, SC can vary widely depending on the issue, parts needed, and the specific brand. Minor repairs like a sensor replacement might range from $150 to $350, while a more complex control valve repair or resin bed replacement could be between $400 and $800 or more. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after diagnosing the problem.

    Some softeners keep a small amount of water in the brine tank by design. Excess standing water typically means a clogged injector, a kinked brine line or a stuck float valve.

    High-purity evaporated salt pellets tend to bridge less and leave less residue than rock salt. Potassium chloride works too but costs more and may require a setting adjustment.

    Most repairs take one to two hours including testing. Resin replacement may take two to three hours depending on the size of the tank.
