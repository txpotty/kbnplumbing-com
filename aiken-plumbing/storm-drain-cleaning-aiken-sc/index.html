    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Aiken

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during heavy rain.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Aiken, SC

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for rehabilitation or replacement.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Aiken

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in Aiken typically ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity of the clog, the size of the drain, and the equipment required. More complex blockages or those requiring extensive hydro jetting may be at the higher end of this range. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    We use water jetters, vacuum equipment and push cameras built for drain work. These tools remove debris without damaging thin-wall storm pipe.

    While you can remove surface debris from drain grates, deeply clogged storm drains require professional tools like hydro jetters and camera equipment for effective cleaning. DIY attempts can sometimes push debris further down or damage the pipes, leading to more costly repairs. It is safer and more effective to hire a professional.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is very effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    Usually, routine storm drain cleaning does not require special permits in Aiken. However, if the cleaning uncovers a need for significant repair, excavation, or connection to public storm sewer lines, then permits from the local municipality or water authority may be necessary. We will advise you if any permits are required for your situation.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may benefit from twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.
