    Main Water Line Repair in Aiken

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Aiken, SC

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Aiken, SC

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Aiken

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Aiken, SC

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Aiken
