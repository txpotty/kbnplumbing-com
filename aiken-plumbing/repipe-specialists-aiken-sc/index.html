    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Aiken

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Aiken

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water.
