    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Aiken

    Septic tank leaks can lead to foul odors, soggy spots in your yard and potential groundwater contamination. Our technicians use dye testing and camera inspections to locate the source of the leak, whether it is a crack in the tank or a failed pipe connection. We provide durable repair solutions using purpose-made sealants and patching materials designed for the harsh environment of a septic tank in Aiken, creating a watertight seal and preventing future issues.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Aiken

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Septic Tank Alarm Troubleshooting in Aiken

    Many modern septic systems, especially those with pumps or advanced treatment units, include alarms to warn of high water levels or system malfunctions. If your septic alarm is sounding in Aiken, it is a clear sign of trouble. KBN Plumbing technicians can quickly troubleshoot the cause of the alarm, whether it is a float switch issue, pump failure or a clogged line, and perform the needed repairs to silence the alarm and restore system balance.

    Leach Field Repair in Aiken, SC

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can signal a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids. We jet clean lines, replace distribution boxes and assess whether the field can be restored. If replacement is needed, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Aiken, SC

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms.
