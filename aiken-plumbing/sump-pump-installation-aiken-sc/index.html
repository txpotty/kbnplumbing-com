    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Aiken

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    KBN Plumbing installs a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Aiken home.

    While DIY sump pump installation is possible, it is not recommended. Proper installation involves important steps like correct pit excavation, discharge line routing, electrical wiring and code compliance. Professional installation by KBN Plumbing means your system works effectively, preventing costly water damage in your Aiken home.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned once a year, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how frequently it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.
