    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Goose Creek, SC

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Goose Creek

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Goose Creek

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Goose Creek take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Goose Creek

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Warm weather for most of the year means water heaters, irrigation lines and outdoor fixtures in South Carolina see year-round use with little seasonal rest. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Goose Creek

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Most South Carolina homes sit on crawlspaces or slabs rather than basements, which puts supply and drain lines either under the floor or under concrete. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Goose Creek, SC

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.
