    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Goose Creek

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Goose Creek, SC

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Goose Creek, SC

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Goose Creek, SC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Goose Creek, SC

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Goose Creek, SC

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.
