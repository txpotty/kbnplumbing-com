    Pipe Bursting in Goose Creek, SC

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Goose Creek

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Goose Creek that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Goose Creek, SC

    Sewer lines in Goose Creek, SC can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Goose Creek

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Sectional Point Repair in Goose Creek

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
