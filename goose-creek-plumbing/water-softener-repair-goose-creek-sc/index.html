    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Goose Creek, SC

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Goose Creek

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used.     Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Goose Creek

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Goose Creek

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Goose Creek, SC

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
