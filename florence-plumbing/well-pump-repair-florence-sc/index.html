    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Florence, SC

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Florence, SC

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. KBN Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Florence

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. New pumps match your well's yield.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Florence, SC

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. The drive, sensor and wiring get tested, and the failed part gets repaired or replaced before we verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Florence

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.
