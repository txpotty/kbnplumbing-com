    How We Replace Polybutylene in Florence, SC

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Florence, SC

    Some homeowners in Florence, SC want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Florence

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Hard freezes are infrequent in South Carolina, but a few nights each winter can drop well below freezing, especially in the upstate. Close the main if a line bursts, and call us to stop it, patch it and get water back on. Then we plan the full fix. Waiting for the next burst is the most expensive way to deal with polybutylene.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Florence, SC

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Florence

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Florence

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Florence

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Florence, SC

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Sometimes it has to wait. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. You will know exactly what remains.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Florence

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.
