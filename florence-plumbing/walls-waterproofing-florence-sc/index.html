    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Florence

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Florence, SC

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Warm weather for most of the year means water heaters, irrigation lines and outdoor fixtures in South Carolina see year-round use with little seasonal rest. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Florence, SC basement.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Florence, SC

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Florence

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area walls.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Florence

    Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s may still have gray polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail and are worth replacing. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks.     Drainage System Integration in Florence, SC

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Florence, SC properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Florence

    Many homes in Florence have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Florence, SC

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves.
