    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Florence, SC

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Florence

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Florence, SC

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner.     Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before we seal anything, and for larger growth, generally more than about ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. We remove smaller affected areas ourselves.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and poor grading or a downspout dumping at the corner adds more, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Moving one sprinkler head can fix it.

    Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on weather and how deep the footing sits. Your timeline comes with the quote.

    Most systems we install should go 20 to 30 years or longer. Life span comes down to the method we use, the quality of the install and the soil conditions around your house. Sump pumps wear out sooner. Most pumps last seven to ten years.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years.
