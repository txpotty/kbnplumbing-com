    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Florence

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Florence, SC

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Florence, SC home.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Florence, SC

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, so we run a camera through the line to find cracks, separated joints and bellies. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Florence home.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Florence

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Florence homes.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Florence, SC

    Older homes in Florence, SC often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Listening Through Concrete in Florence

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, and that skill only comes with lots of practice. Pressurized lines are the easiest to hear in your Florence home.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Florence

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Florence, SC home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Florence

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.
