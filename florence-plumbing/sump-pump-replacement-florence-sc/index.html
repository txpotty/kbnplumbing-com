    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Florence

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Florence

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps generally cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is important for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    KBN Plumbing provides warranty coverage on both the sump pump unit itself, per the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship. We back our installations for your peace of mind and a reliable, long-lasting solution.

    While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Florence codes.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.
