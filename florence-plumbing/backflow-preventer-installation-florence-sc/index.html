    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Florence

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Florence, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Florence

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Florence. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. KBN Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Florence, SC

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Before a forecast freeze, disconnecting garden hoses, covering hose bibs and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to protect a home. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Florence, SC

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Florence, SC, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Florence

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water utility.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Florence

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.
