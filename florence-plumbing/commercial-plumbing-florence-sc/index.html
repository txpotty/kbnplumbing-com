    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Florence

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Florence

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Florence, SC

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Florence, SC

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Florence, SC

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Grease Trap Installation in Florence
