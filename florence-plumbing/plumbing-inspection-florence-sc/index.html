    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Florence, SC

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails.     Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Florence, SC

    High humidity accelerates corrosion on exposed copper and fittings in crawlspaces, so a quick look under the house is worth doing every year. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.
