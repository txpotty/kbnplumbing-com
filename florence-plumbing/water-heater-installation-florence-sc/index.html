    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Florence, SC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank.     Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Florence

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Florence, SC

    Expansion Tank Installation in Florence, SC

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Florence, SC

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.
