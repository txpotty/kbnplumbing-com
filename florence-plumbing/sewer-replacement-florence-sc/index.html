    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Florence

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe.     Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Florence

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most effective solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure thorough planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Florence, SC

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Florence, SC

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are likely to fail. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Florence

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Florence

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Florence will conduct a complete camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution.
