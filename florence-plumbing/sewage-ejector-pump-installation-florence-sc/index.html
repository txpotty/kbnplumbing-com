    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Florence

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Florence usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000. This price can vary depending on the pump type, basin size, complexity of the plumbing and electrical work and whether an existing system needs to be removed. We provide a detailed quote after assessing your project.

    New ejector installations usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for every job.

    Most ejector pumps last 7 to 10 years with normal use. Flushing wipes or other non-flushable items can shorten that life considerably.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause premature failure.

    A sewage ejector pump is designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, removes groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve different purposes for different types of water.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. KBN Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional check your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.
