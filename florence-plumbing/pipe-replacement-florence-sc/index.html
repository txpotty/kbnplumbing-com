    What Goes Into a Repipe in Florence, SC

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Florence

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    PEX Repiping in Florence

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Florence

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, which skips the jackhammer. We explain both.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Florence, SC

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Florence, SC

    Before a forecast freeze, disconnecting garden hoses, covering hose bibs and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to protect a home. Ice inside a pipe cracks it open. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Florence, SC

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Florence

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, so only part of the building is offline at a time. Night work available.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Florence, SC

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.
