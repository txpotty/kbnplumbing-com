    While you can perform basic checks like making sure the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper. Our technicians can identify subtle electrical issues, float switch malfunctions or impeller damage that a DIY check might miss. We recommend professional service for thoroughness and safety.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment typically takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run a little longer.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing matters during dry months.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    The check valve is crucial because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and higher energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.
