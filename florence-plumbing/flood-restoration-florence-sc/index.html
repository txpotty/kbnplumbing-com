    Frequently Asked Questions About Flood Restoration in Florence

    The cost of flood restoration in Florence, SC can vary a great deal depending on the extent of the damage, the size of the affected area, and the type of water, whether clean, gray, or black water. Minor water damage might range from $1,500 to $4,000, while extensive flooding requiring structural drying and repairs could cost upwards of $10,000 to $30,000. We provide detailed, upfront estimates.

    Signs of hidden water damage include musty odors, peeling paint or wallpaper, visible mold spots, warped flooring, or discolored walls or ceilings. If you suspect hidden moisture, our thermal imaging and moisture detection tools can accurately pinpoint affected areas that are not visible to the naked eye.

    Mold prevention is a key part of our process. We achieve this by rapid water extraction, thorough drying to reduce humidity and moisture levels, and applying EPA-approved antimicrobial treatments to all affected porous materials. Controlling moisture within the first 24 to 48 hours is key for inhibiting mold spores.

    Permits are usually not required for basic water extraction and drying. However, if the flood damage calls for significant structural repairs, electrical work, or plumbing system overhauls, permits from Florence authorities may be required. KBN Plumbing will advise you on any required permits and can help coordinate the application process.

    While you can handle very small, clean water spills, professional flood restoration is highly recommended for serious water damage. Professionals have the specialized equipment, expertise, and certifications to properly extract water, thoroughly dry hidden areas, prevent mold, and make sure your property is safe and structurally sound. DIY efforts often miss hidden moisture, leading to lasting problems.

    Water removal and drying usually take three to seven days depending on the extent of flooding. Rebuild work can take additional days or weeks. We give you a timeline after the initial assessment.

    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover water damage from sudden and accidental events, like a burst pipe or water heater leak. However, damage from natural floods usually requires a separate flood insurance policy. We can help you navigate the claims process and provide documentation for your insurer.
