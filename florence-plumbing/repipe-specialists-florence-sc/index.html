    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Florence

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Florence

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Florence

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through.     Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Florence, SC

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    Drain Line Repiping in Florence

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. A camera shows the condition. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    PEX Repiping in Florence, SC

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Florence

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Florence

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.
