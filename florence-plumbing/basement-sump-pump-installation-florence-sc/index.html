    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible.     New Sump Pit Excavation in Florence

    No pit yet? First we locate the low point where water gathers, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. We pour new concrete flush with the floor.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Florence

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Florence, SC

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Florence, SC we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Sump Pit Installation in Florence, SC

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Florence

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Florence. Each cycle, the panel switches which pump leads, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Florence

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. A brand new pit through the slab falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. Most people add the backup.

    Submersibles sit in the pit, sealed, and run quiet. A pedestal mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes them cheaper and easier to service but louder, so they work best in an unfinished utility room rather than a finished family room. Either type gets the job done.

    Permit rules vary by town, including Florence. Replacing a pump in the same pit rarely needs one, but a new pit, a new outlet or a new exit through the foundation wall often does, and we sort that out before any concrete gets cut. The paperwork is on us.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. The pump carries its own manufacturer warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Keep the receipt.

    Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps cover most of our installs. We default to cast iron.
