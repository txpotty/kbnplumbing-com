    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. Pressure comes back right away.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Florence, SC

    Pipe Bursting Services in Florence, SC

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Florence

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, so concrete and established plantings stay put. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Florence

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. We quote after inspection.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, and every one of these holds up to soil and water far better than galvanized or clay. We follow your city's code.

    Check the meter with every fixture off. Look at the leak indicator. If it moves, water is going somewhere, and a wet or sinking spot in the yard or a hissing sound near the meter box usually points to the buried service. We can confirm it fast.

    Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Each is approved for direct bury. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, and the final choice depends on the existing pipe, the soil and what your local code allows. We match transitions carefully.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Read the exclusions. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, while slow leaks, wear and tear and old pipe failing from age usually are not. Call your agent to confirm. We provide documentation for the claim.

    We use PEX from major manufacturers like Uponor and Viega. Pipe meets ASTM standards. PVC and ABS products meet the ASTM specs for buried water or drain service, and fittings are rated for direct burial so they hold up to soil contact and moisture for decades. No off-brand fittings.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Main lines are not. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marks and code-compliant fittings rated for burial, so those should go to a licensed plumber. Utilities have to be marked before digging.

    Main water service and sewer replacements usually do. Small fixes may not. The Florence building department typically wants a permit for any major buried line replacement, which allows an inspection before backfill. The permit is our job.
