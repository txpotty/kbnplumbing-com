    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Summerville, SC

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Summerville

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Summerville, SC

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Summerville, SC homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
