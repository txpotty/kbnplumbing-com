    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Summerville, SC

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs land between $250 and $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor.     Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Summerville, SC

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Summerville

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, check for a crossover valve under the far sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Summerville, SC

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Summerville, SC

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. Our tech cleans the burner and fan, checks every vent joint, and compares the flow sensor reading to the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Summerville

    Restaurants and laundromats in Summerville often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. When one unit drops out, the rest carry the load and scale up sooner, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. Downtime costs money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Summerville

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.
