    Recurring Clog Repair in Summerville

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Summerville, SC

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Red clay dominates much of the upstate, while the coastal plain of South Carolina has sandy soil and a high water table that affects drainage and digging. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Summerville

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. That decides the repair.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Summerville

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.
