    Gas and Electric Water Heaters in Summerville, SC

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Summerville

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Summerville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Summerville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Summerville, SC

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Summerville, SC

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Summerville

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Summerville, SC

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Summerville, SC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.
