    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Summerville

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Summerville

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. KBN Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a reliable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Summerville, SC.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Summerville

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Summerville. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for easier installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Summerville, SC

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.
