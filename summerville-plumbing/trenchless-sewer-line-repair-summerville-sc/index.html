    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Summerville

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Summerville homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Summerville, SC

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter.     Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Summerville, SC

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by South Carolina homeowners.
