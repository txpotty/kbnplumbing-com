    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Summerville

    The cost for sump pump maintenance in Summerville, SC usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the pump type and its condition. This price usually includes a thorough inspection, cleaning and operational testing. Any necessary repairs or parts replacement would be an additional cost, which we would quote upfront.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This shows the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a wise choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    The check valve is essential because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and higher energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.

    Routine sump pump maintenance, which involves inspection, cleaning and testing, typically does not require a permit in Summerville, SC. However, if the service involves significant changes to the plumbing system or a full replacement, a permit may be necessary. We will let you if a permit is needed for your situation.

    Key signs include strange noises like grinding or rattling, the pump running constantly, failing to turn on, visible rust or corrosion, or water pooling in your basement. Don't wait for a flood to contact us.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another approved location.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also red flag signs.
