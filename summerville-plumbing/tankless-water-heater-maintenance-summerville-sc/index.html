    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Summerville

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Summerville

    Proper ventilation is essential for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Summerville includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Summerville, SC

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their solid build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to avoid damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a frequent area for attention in Summerville.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Summerville

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Summerville

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Summerville is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, providing recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Summerville

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and ensure proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues early, improving performance for your Summerville home.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Summerville, SC

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Summerville, SC

    Noritz tankless water heaters are robust systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, confirming proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger.
