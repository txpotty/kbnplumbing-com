    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Summerville

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Summerville for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Summerville

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by KBN Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Summerville codes.
