    Expansion Tank Replacement in Summerville, SC

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.
