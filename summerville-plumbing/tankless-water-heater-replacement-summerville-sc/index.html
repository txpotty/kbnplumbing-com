    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Summerville

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Summerville, SC

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Hard freezes are infrequent in South Carolina, but a few nights each winter can drop well below freezing, especially in the upstate. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Summerville

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. That keeps wall patching small. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Summerville

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Summerville, SC

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Summerville, SC

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. Installs can happen after closing.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Summerville

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Summerville

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.
