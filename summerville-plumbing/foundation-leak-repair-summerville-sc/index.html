    Foundation Leak Detection in Summerville

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Summerville, SC

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Summerville

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these hidden leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, providing a durable repair for your Summerville property.

    Water Line Reroute in Summerville

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Summerville

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or poor installation. KBN Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use correct fusion and solvent welding techniques to make strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Summerville foundation.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Summerville

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Summerville, SC

    Many older homes in Summerville, SC have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run fairly soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.
