    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Greenville, SC

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Greenville

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Greenville, SC

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Greenville, SC

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more.     Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Greenville, SC

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow kitchen sinks often mean the trap is full or the baffles are gone. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Greenville, SC

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property.
