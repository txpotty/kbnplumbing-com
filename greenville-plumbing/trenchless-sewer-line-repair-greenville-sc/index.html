    Root Intrusion Repair in Greenville, SC

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Greenville, SC

    Sewer lines in Greenville, SC can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Greenville

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Pipe Bursting in Greenville, SC

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Greenville, SC

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by South Carolina homeowners.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Greenville

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
