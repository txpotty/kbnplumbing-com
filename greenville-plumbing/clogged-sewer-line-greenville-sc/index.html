    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Greenville

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints.     Main Line Hydro Jetting in Greenville

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Greenville, SC

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. We also share simple habits that help prevent another grease or wipe clog.
