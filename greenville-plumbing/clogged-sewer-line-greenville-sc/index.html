    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Greenville, SC

    Most South Carolina homes sit on crawlspaces or slabs rather than basements, which puts supply and drain lines either under the floor or under concrete. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Water and solids sit in the dip. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Greenville

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Greenville, SC

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.
