    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Greenville, SC

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. KBN Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Greenville

    Scale on faucets means trouble. KBN Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Greenville home. A hardness test strip tells the story in seconds, and we run one before the repair and again after so you can see the drop in grains per gallon.

    Resin Bed Problems in Greenville

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Greenville, SC

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Greenville

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Greenville, SC

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Greenville, SC

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.
