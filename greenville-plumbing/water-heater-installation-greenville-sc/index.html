    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Greenville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Greenville, SC

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Greenville, SC

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Greenville, SC

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Greenville, SC

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Greenville, SC

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Permits are included. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    Water heater installation in Greenville, SC runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. The biggest factors are whether it is a tank or tankless unit, its size, its fuel and whether the gas line or panel needs work. Tankless sits at the high end. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    Tank and parts coverage usually runs 6 to 12 years. Our labor has its own warranty.

    Start with correct sizing and a thermostat set at 120 degrees. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe and flushing the tank once a year both help, and a tankless model cuts standby loss.

    Tank water heaters last about 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Flushing and anode checks extend life.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It keeps pressure spikes off your plumbing.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless or relocation work takes longer.
