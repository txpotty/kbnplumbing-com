    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Greenville, SC

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Greenville, SC

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Greenville, SC

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. KBN Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Greenville.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Greenville

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Greenville, SC

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team ensures all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, enhancing your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more water-wise models.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Greenville, SC

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on track.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Greenville

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.
