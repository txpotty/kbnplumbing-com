    New Sump Pit Installation in Greenville

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Greenville, SC

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Greenville

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Greenville, SC

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Greenville, SC

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Greenville

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Greenville

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.
