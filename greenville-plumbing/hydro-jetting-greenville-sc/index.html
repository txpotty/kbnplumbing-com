    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Greenville

    Residential hydro jetting in Greenville usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary.     It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    The duration of a hydro jetting service in Greenville varies depending on the severity and location of the clog. A typical residential drain might take 1 to 2 hours, while a major sewer line or commercial job could take 3 to 4 hours or more. Our technicians work efficiently to minimize disruption.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are solid reasons to consider jetting.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow.
