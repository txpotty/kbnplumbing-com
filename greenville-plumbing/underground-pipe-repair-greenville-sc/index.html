    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Greenville

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, which saves sod, irrigation, pavers and concrete that would otherwise have to be torn out and replaced. Faster, too.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Greenville

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Greenville, SC

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Underground Water Line Repair in Greenville, SC

    A leaking water service runs at full street pressure around the clock. Bills climb fast. Repairs use approved fittings.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Greenville, SC

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Old pipe gives up. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets South Carolina plumbing code.
