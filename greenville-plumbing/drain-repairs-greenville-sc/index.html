    The cost of drain repairs in Greenville, SC can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs.     Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your drains, recurring clogs or water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is smart to call a professional before the problem worsens.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.

    The length of drain repairs depends on the complexity of the problem. Simple clog removals might take an hour or two, while more extensive repairs like replacing a section of a main sewer line could take a full day or more. We'll give you an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets.
