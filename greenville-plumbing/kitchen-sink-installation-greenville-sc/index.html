    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Greenville

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Greenville, SC

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.
