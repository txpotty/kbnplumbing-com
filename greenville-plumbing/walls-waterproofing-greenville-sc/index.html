    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Greenville

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Greenville, SC can vary significantly based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide clear, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    We use a range of high-performance waterproofing membranes and coatings depending on the application and foundation type. These can include liquid asphalt-modified polyurethane, rubberized asphalt membranes, bentonite clay systems, and crystalline coatings. Brands often include trusted names known for their durability and effectiveness in preventing water penetration.

    KBN Plumbing works with trusted manufacturers known for high-quality waterproofing products. We commonly use products from brands like Xypex, Tremco, and Sika for their proven effectiveness and durability, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. We select the best product for your foundation type and site.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects that involve excavation, major structural changes, or changes to drainage systems, a permit from the Greenville building department may be required. We will advise you on any required permits and help with the application process so all work complies with local codes.

    Walls waterproofing is a primary step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems built to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is essential.

    Look for signs such as damp or wet spots on basement or crawl space walls, a musty odor, visible mold or mildew growth, efflorescence, a white powdery residue, on concrete or brick, and standing water. Cracks in foundation walls or bowing walls also point to potential water entry issues. If you notice any of these, it's wise to get a professional inspection.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.
