    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Mount Pleasant

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
