    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Mount Pleasant

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Mount Pleasant, SC

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Mount Pleasant homes.

    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Mount Pleasant

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and ensure the resin bed is working effectively. This preventive service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Mount Pleasant, SC

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Mount Pleasant

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Mount Pleasant.
