    Repair or Replace a Softener in Mount Pleasant, SC

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Resin Rebedding in Mount Pleasant

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Mount Pleasant

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Mount Pleasant

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.
