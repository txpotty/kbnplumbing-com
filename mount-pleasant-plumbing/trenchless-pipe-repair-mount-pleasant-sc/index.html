    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Mount Pleasant

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Mount Pleasant yard.
