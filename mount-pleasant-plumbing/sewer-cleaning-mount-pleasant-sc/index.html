    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Mount Pleasant

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common culprits in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can decide on lining or repair.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.
