    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Mount Pleasant

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Mount Pleasant

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes.     Water Pressure Restoration in Mount Pleasant

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Mount Pleasant

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.
