    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Mount Pleasant

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Mount Pleasant. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Mount Pleasant

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often run $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Mount Pleasant, SC, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.
