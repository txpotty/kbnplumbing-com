    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s.     Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap.
