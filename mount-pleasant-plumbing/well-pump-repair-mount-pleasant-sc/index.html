    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. KBN Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Mount Pleasant operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.
