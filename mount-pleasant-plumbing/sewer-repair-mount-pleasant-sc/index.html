    What Sewer Repair Covers in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Root Intrusion Removal in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Mount Pleasant yards make this a common call.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Mount Pleasant

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Mount Pleasant

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Mount Pleasant, SC

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Mount Pleasant

    Every repair starts with a look inside. We run the camera from the cleanout. You get a copy of the video and a plain written summary of each defect with its distance from the house, which helps if you are buying a home, filing a claim or getting a second opinion on a quote someone else gave you. Sometimes the answer is that the line is fine.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your South Carolina property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mount Pleasant

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.
