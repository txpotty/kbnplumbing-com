    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Mount Pleasant, SC

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mount Pleasant

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. KBN Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We accurately locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to minimize disruption.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. KBN Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Mount Pleasant, SC
