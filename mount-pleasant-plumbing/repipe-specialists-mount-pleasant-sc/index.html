    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Mount Pleasant

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Copper Repiping in Mount Pleasant, SC

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Mount Pleasant, SC

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
