    Anode Rod Checks in Greer

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Greer

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Greer, SC

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.
