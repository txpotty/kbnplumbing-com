    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Greer

    Most Greer, SC softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. The price usually includes the equipment and labor, with extra plumbing shown as its own line. Bigger homes cost more. We give written pricing up front.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. If pressure falls after the install, it usually points to an undersized valve or a clogged pre-filter, which our plumbers can fix.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    Every one to two months is typical, depending on water use and hardness. A metered valve stretches each bag.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Do not let the salt run out.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring a pro. Bad installs cause leaks, poor softening or slow damage to the plumbing that nobody notices until a fitting lets go behind the water heater. Professional work gets sizing, connections and code right.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. A family of four with fairly hard water might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month, and a metered high-efficiency model uses less. Metered valves save most.
