    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Greer

    The cost of water softener installation in Greer, SC can range from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the type and size of the system, your home's existing plumbing and any necessary modifications. This typically includes the unit itself and the labor for professional installation. We provide written, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Greer home to verify your need for a softener.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Many homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking water.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could indicate an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more effective for very hard water.

    Maintenance primarily involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness. It's also good practice to schedule annual check-ups with a professional plumber to ensure the system is operating efficiently and to address any potential issues.
