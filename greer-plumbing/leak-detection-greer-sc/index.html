    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Greer, SC

    Gas Leak Detection in Greer

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Greer

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector.     Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Greer, SC

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Greer

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most.     Preventive Leak Inspections in Greer, SC

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves.     Pool Leak Detection in Greer
