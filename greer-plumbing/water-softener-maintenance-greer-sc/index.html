    Control Valve and Injector Service in Greer, SC

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Greer, SC

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Greer, SC

Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity.
