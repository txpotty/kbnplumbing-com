    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Greer

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Greer, SC can range from $300 to $800 or more, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe.     Key signs you might need hydrojet plumbing include frequently recurring clogs that snaking doesn't fully resolve, multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, or foul odors coming from your drains. A severe sewer backup is also a clear indicator that hydrojetting is needed to clear the main line.

    Hydrojetting is very effective against stubborn clogs caused by grease buildup, mineral scale, soap scum, hair, and even tree root intrusions. It can clear almost any type of blockage that accumulates inside a pipe, restoring full flow.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, usually leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more complete and longer-lasting solution.

    We guarantee the line will flow freely when we leave, and our service warranty is written on your invoice. If structural damage is found, we explain it before work starts so you know what jetting can and cannot fix. That keeps expectations realistic.

    The duration of hydrojet plumbing depends on the complexity of the clog and the length of the pipe being cleaned.     It is safe for most cast iron, clay, ABS and PVC lines in reasonable condition. We always inspect with a camera first and lower the pressure or recommend another method if the pipe is cracked or badly corroded. That step protects fragile lines.
