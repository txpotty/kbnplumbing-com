    What Our Gas Leak Repairs Cover in Greer, SC

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Greer

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. The tag usually says what they found, so we start there. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day or two.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Greer

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Greer, SC

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. The first-stage regulator at the tank and the second-stage regulator at the house both get checked. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with buried tanks get a line test too.

    Emergency Gas Leak Services in Greer

    That sulfur smell is mercaptan, an odorant the supplier adds because natural gas and propane have almost no smell on their own. If the smell is strong, walk out and leave the door open behind you. Do not flip switches. After the utility shuts the meter, our tech comes out for the repair. We bring fittings, pipe and connectors on the truck, so most leaks are fixed in one trip.

    Gas Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Greer, SC

    Price follows access. Expect $150 to $450 for a connector or fitting repair you can reach, with the leak test included.     Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Greer, SC

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Badly rusted sections, often near a crawlspace vent, are cut back to clean pipe and replaced. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Greer, SC

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Greer, SC

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade. A new riser or a new buried run gets tested and left ready for the utility to turn on. Expect a half day for most riser swap.
