    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Greer

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Greer

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Greer

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Greer, SC

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. They leave on our truck. The old cord, any replaced mount ring and the rusty slip nuts go too, so you are not stuck with a bag of parts on trash day. Most replacements finish in one visit.

    Evolution Series Installation in Greer

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. Tight cabinets suit the Compact, while the Excel wants the most space.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Greer, SC

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.
