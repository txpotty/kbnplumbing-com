    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Greer

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run fairly soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Yes, a plumbing permit is usually required for a whole-home repipe in Greer, SC. This confirms the work complies with local building codes and safety standards.
