    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Greer

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Greer for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Greer

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Greer home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Greer

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Greer

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Greer, SC

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. KBN Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Greer, SC so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Greer

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Greer, SC

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Greer, SC, with proper alignment and secure mounting.
