    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in North Charleston

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in North Charleston, SC

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off.     Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. Loose carriers crack bowls. Flushometer valves get reconnected and tested before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in North Charleston

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting.     Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in North Charleston

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in North Charleston

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Epoxy will not hold. We replace the bowl and use bolts snugged by hand, then finish with a quarter turn so the porcelain is not stressed again.
