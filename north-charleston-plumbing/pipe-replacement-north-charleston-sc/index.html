    What Goes Into a Repipe in North Charleston, SC

    PEX Pipe Installation in North Charleston

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Private wells are common outside town limits, and some South Carolina well water carries iron, low pH or sediment that can stain fixtures and wear down water heaters. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in North Charleston

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in North Charleston, SC

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Whole-Home Repiping in North Charleston

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, finishing with smooth patches ready for paint. Clean finish.

    PEX Repiping in North Charleston

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in North Charleston

    Apartments and condos share risers. Shared lines mean shared problems. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in North Charleston

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.
