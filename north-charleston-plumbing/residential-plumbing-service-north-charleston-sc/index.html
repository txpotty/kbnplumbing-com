    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in North Charleston, SC

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. We repipe with PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping in North Charleston, SC

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition.     Water Softener and Filtration Service in North Charleston

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Two leaks in a year is the tipping point for most homes. Polybutylene and galvanized pipe also tilt the answer toward a repipe, since patching one spot on a failing system just moves the next leak a few feet down the line. We price both side by side.

    One to two hours covers many repairs. Plan on half a day for a water heater swap, and a whole-house repipe runs a few days, with the water turned back on each evening so the family can shower and cook. The schedule is set before we start.

    Swapping a showerhead or a toilet flapper is fine for most people. Gas lines, water heaters and anything behind a wall belong with a licensed plumber, for safety and because those jobs usually need a permit and an inspection that a homeowner repair can fail. Plumber's tape will not fix a gas leak.

    Every repair comes with a written parts and labor warranty. We also register the manufacturer warranty on new fixtures and heaters for you, which matters because some water heater makers shorten the tank warranty if the unit is never registered. Save the serial number.

    Some jobs are fine to DIY. Swapping a flapper or clearing a sink trap is well within reach, but gas lines, water heaters and anything inside a wall can turn into a flood, a fire risk or a failed inspection if the work is off. A licensed plumber gets those right and to code.

    Watch for drips that will not stop, slow drains, banging or whistling pipes, a sudden drop in pressure, discolored water and wet spots with no obvious source. Each one is a symptom of something that will get worse. Do not ignore them.
