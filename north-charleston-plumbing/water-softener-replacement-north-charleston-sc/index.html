    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in North Charleston, SC

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in South Carolina homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in North Charleston, SC

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Urgent Softener Problems in North Charleston

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in North Charleston

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in North Charleston, SC

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Brine Tank Replacement in North Charleston

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in North Charleston, SC

    Swapping a salt-based softener in South Carolina homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.
