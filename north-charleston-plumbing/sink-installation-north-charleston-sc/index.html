    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in North Charleston

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in North Charleston, SC

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Vessel Sink Installation in North Charleston, SC

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in North Charleston, SC

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in North Charleston

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
