    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can catch potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    KBN Plumbing offers a solid warranty on all sewage ejector pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The warranty period can vary based on the pump manufacturer's warranty and our service guarantee. We will provide full details on your coverage before starting any work.

    A standard sewage ejector pump installation usually takes one to two days.     Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves occasionally checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch.
