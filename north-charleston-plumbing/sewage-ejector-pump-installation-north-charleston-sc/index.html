    What Goes Into an Ejector System in North Charleston, SC

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in North Charleston, SC

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Parts are easy to find.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in North Charleston, SC

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in North Charleston, SC

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. Cracked basins get replaced.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in North Charleston

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. They need a vent too.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in North Charleston

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in North Charleston, SC

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in North Charleston

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Coastal areas deal with storm surge and a high water table, while inland areas see flash flooding from slow-moving summer thunderstorms. Battery backup is an option.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in North Charleston

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.
