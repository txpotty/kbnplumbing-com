    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in North Charleston

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in North Charleston, SC

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in North Charleston, SC

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Emergency Sink Repair in North Charleston, SC

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in North Charleston

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. A leaking unit is another matter. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in North Charleston, SC

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in North Charleston

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
