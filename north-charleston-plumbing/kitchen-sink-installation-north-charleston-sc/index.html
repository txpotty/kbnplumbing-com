    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in North Charleston, SC: The Details

    Apron-Front Sinks in North Charleston, SC

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in North Charleston

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in North Charleston

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in North Charleston, SC

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in North Charleston, SC

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in North Charleston

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in North Charleston

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can flood a cabinet within hours. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. If you have a standard 33 by 22 inch drop-in, we can usually remove the damaged sink and set a new one that day.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in North Charleston

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.
