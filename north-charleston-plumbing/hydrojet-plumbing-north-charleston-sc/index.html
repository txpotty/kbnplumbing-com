    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in North Charleston, SC

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in North Charleston

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in North Charleston, SC

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in North Charleston, SC

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Residential Hydrojetting in North Charleston

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in North Charleston, SC

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in North Charleston

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in North Charleston

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Hydrojet Plumbing Near Me in North Charleston, SC
