    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in North Charleston, SC

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in North Charleston

    Electric water heaters in North Charleston usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in North Charleston, SC homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in North Charleston

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in North Charleston, SC

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Leaks only get worse.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in North Charleston, SC

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in North Charleston, SC. Tankless and hybrid heat pump models cost more to buy and install but can cut operating costs over the life of the unit, and tankless units often last 15 to 20 years compared to roughly 8 to 12 for a typical tank. We price all three.
