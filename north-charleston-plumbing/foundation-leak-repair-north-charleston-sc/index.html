    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in North Charleston, SC

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in North Charleston

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. KBN Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your North Charleston foundation is sound again.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in North Charleston

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Radar scanning maps the cables before any cut, and when the leak is too close to a tendon, we reroute the line instead.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in North Charleston, SC

    Basement walls in North Charleston, SC leak through cracks, tie holes and the joint where the wall meets the floor. Cracks get injected.
