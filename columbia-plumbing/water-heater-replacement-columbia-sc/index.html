    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Columbia

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap.     Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. Hot water by evening.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Columbia, SC

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Businesses in Columbia put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery.     Attic Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Much of the housing in South Carolina was built after 1970, so copper, CPVC and PEX supply lines are far more common than old galvanized pipe.
