    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Columbia

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary.     Pipe Bursting Services in Columbia, SC

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a robust solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Columbia, SC.
