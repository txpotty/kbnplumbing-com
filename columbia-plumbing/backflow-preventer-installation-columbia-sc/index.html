    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Columbia

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Columbia. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Columbia, SC

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house. A pressure vacuum breaker stops back-siphonage on most residential systems, and we mount it at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head so it can do its job in Columbia, SC. Height is the code detail people miss.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Columbia

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. We size for flow.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Columbia, SC
