    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Columbia

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    To keep your new sump pump running well, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Annually, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from KBN Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Columbia home.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before the rainy season helps catch problems early.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, ideally before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent surprise failures.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.
