    Cast iron rots from the inside. Older homes in Columbia and historic districts often have cast iron drains and galvanized supply pipe that corrode from the inside after decades of service.     Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Columbia

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Columbia, SC

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust.
