    Dependable Rooter Services in Columbia, SC

    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Columbia, SC

    Kitchen drains collect grease, soap and food that harden into stubborn plugs. We reach the line through the trap or a cleanout and use a medium cable with a spiral cutter. For very heavy grease, a follow-up jetting pass scrubs the pipe walls clean. This approach keeps the drain flowing well longer.

    Rooter Plus Hydro Jetting in Columbia

    Rooting cuts through a clog, while jetting scours the inside of the pipe. Combining the two clears heavy roots and grease more thoroughly than either one by itself. We camera the line before jetting to make sure the pipe can handle the pressure. This combination is popular for restaurants and homes with recurring root problems.

    Sewer Root Removal in Columbia

    Tree roots are the leading cause of main sewer clogs in many established neighborhoods. Roots from shade trees and thirsty landscape plants enter through tiny gaps in clay and cast iron joints and grow into thick mats. We run a heavy sewer machine with a root saw head to cut them back to the pipe wall. Regular rooting every 12 to 24 months keeps most lines open.

    Toilet and Bathroom Rooter Service in Columbia

    Toilets clog from too much paper, wipes and objects dropped by kids. We use a closet auger designed to protect the porcelain and, when needed, pull the toilet to root the line beneath it. Bathroom sinks and tubs get a smaller cable that removes hair and soap buildup. We reset any fixture we remove with a new wax ring or seal.

    Camera Inspection for Rooter Services in Columbia, SC

    For difficult or recurring clogs, KBN Plumbing uses plumbing camera inspections as part of our rooter services in Columbia, SC. This allows us to see the exact location and nature of the blockage, whether it's roots, a collapsed pipe, or a foreign object. This accurate diagnosis means we apply the most effective clearing method, avoiding guesswork and unnecessary digging.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Columbia, SC

    For the most stubborn clogs and extensive pipe buildup, KBN Plumbing offers hydro-jetting services in Columbia, SC. This technique uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of your pipes, removing grease, mineral deposits, and even tree roots. Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run fairly soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. Hydro-jetting cleans pipes more thoroughly than standard snaking, restoring them to near-original condition and reducing future clogs.

    Rooting vs. Pipe Repair in Columbia, SC

    Rooting is the right fix when the pipe is sound but clogged. If roots keep returning quickly, or the cable meets broken pipe, a repair may save money over time. Rooting usually costs a few hundred dollars, while lining or replacement costs more but ends repeat clogs. A camera inspection shows which option makes sense.

    Main Line Rooter Service Through Cleanouts in Columbia, SC

    The simplest way to root a main line is through an outdoor or garage cleanout. We run cable all the way to the city connection to make sure the entire line is clear. If your home has no accessible cleanout, we can install one to make future service faster. Water is run through the line afterward to confirm full flow.
