    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Columbia, SC

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Columbia, SC use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for optimal performance and safety.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Columbia, SC

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Columbia

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Columbia

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We make sure your setup meets local code.
