    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Columbia, SC

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.
