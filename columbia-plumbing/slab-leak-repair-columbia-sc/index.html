    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Columbia, SC

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Columbia

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Paired with acoustic listening gear, it lets us mark a leak in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe even near busy roads, because the radar fixes the pipe route and the mic confirms the leak. We use both in Columbia.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Columbia

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. For those, an acoustic sensor listens through the concrete for the sound of water escaping, and we mark the spot where it peaks, whether the house is an older ranch or a newer two-story. Both tools ride on every Columbia truck.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Columbia

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Columbia home is no exception.     Slab Leak Detection in Columbia
