    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Columbia, SC Home

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Columbia

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Columbia

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (864) 588-3813 and we will send someone. Leave the lid off, get cords off the wet floor, and run a wet vac to keep the water level below the top of the pit while you wait. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot. We will also figure out what killed the old pump.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Columbia

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Columbia

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Columbia

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.
