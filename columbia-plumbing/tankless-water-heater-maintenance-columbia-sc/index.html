    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Columbia

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Columbia

    Proper ventilation is essential for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Columbia includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We make sure that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Columbia

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Columbia

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump.     Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Columbia, SC
