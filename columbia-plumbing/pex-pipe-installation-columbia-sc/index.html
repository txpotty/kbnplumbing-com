    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Columbia, SC

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Columbia, SC

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Columbia

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it perfect for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Columbia, PEX is the pipe of choice.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Columbia

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.
