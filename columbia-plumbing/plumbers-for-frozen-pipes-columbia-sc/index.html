    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Columbia

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. A frozen drain backs up. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Columbia

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Columbia

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. It has to fit. Seams get taped and corners get mitered, because one bare fitting is often where the freeze starts.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Columbia, SC

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Columbia

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze costs far less than a burst pipe repair. We walk the house and mark every exposed run. We insulate attic lines, cover vents, upgrade outdoor faucets and drain irrigation lines.
