    Pressure Switch Replacement in Columbia

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Columbia

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Columbia home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Columbia, SC

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. A backwater valve, a working sump pump where one exists and clear yard drains go a long way toward protecting a home during storm season. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Columbia

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Columbia.
