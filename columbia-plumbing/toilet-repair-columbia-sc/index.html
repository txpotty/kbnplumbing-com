    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Columbia

    Toilet Flush Repair in Columbia, SC

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Columbia, SC

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Columbia, SC

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff.
