    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Columbia

    Residential ejector systems in Columbia serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Columbia, SC

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Columbia

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours.
