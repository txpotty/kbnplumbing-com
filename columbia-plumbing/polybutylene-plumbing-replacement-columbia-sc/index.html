    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia, SC

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Columbia

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.
