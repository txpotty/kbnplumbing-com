    What We Install in Columbia, SC

    Water Service Line Installation in Columbia, SC

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. We bring the digging equipment the ground calls for. We add a new main shutoff at the entry point. Depth and bedding protect the pipe. Pressure and flow get tested before the trench is closed.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Columbia, SC

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit. Before we quote one, we check that your panel and service can carry that load, and we bring in a licensed electrician when they cannot. The plumbing side is simpler. We set the flow rate and outlet temperature so the shower does not run cold when the dishwasher kicks on.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Columbia

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Columbia, SC

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Columbia, SC

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Softener Loop and Conditioner Installs in Columbia

    Private wells are common outside town limits, and some South Carolina well water carries iron, low pH or sediment that can stain fixtures and wear down water heaters. Some homes already have a softener loop plumbed in, usually in the garage, which turns the install into a two-hour job. Without a loop, we tie into the main and build one. Figure on a few extra hours for that. A salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits the same loop.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Columbia

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Columbia, SC

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. New lines get pressure tested, always. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.
