    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Columbia

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Columbia

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Columbia

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Columbia, SC

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Water Softener Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Columbia

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Columbia, SC

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. KBN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Columbia, SC

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Water softener installation in Columbia, SC usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. System type, brand, capacity and how much plumbing work the install needs all move the price within that range. New loops cost more. We quote after an on-site test.
