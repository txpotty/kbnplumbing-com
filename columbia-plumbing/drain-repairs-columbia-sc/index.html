    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Columbia, SC

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Every repair is done to code.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Columbia

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. That decides the repair.

    Storm Drain Repair in Columbia, SC

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Coastal areas deal with storm surge and a high water table, while inland areas see sudden flooding from slow-moving summer thunderstorms. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. A clean outlet matters too.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Columbia, SC

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom.
