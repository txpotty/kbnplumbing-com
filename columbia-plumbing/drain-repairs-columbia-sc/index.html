    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Columbia

    The cost of drain repairs in Columbia, SC can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all influence the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Minor fixture drain repairs typically do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is commonly used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.
