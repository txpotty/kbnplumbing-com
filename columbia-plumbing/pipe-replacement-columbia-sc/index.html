    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Columbia

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Before a forecast freeze, disconnecting garden hoses, covering hose bibs and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to protect a home. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Columbia, SC

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    PEX Repiping in Columbia

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Columbia, SC

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Columbia, SC

    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.
