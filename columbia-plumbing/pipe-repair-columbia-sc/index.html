    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Columbia

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Columbia

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Columbia

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.
