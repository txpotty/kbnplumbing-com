    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Columbia

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Composite Sink Installation in Columbia

    Composite sinks, made from materials like granite or quartz composites, offer excellent durability and a wide range of colors to match your kitchen decor. Installing these sinks requires careful handling to prevent damage and ensure a perfect fit. Our plumbers in Columbia are experienced with composite sink installation, using the right tools and techniques for secure mounting and sealing. We ensure all drain and faucet connections are tight and leak-free, providing you with a long-lasting and attractive kitchen fixture.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Columbia

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many South Carolina homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still important to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Columbia install top-mount sinks, applying high-quality sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Columbia, SC

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. KBN Plumbing is skilled at under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a fully sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Columbia

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Sink & Garbage Disposal Integration in Columbia, SC

    Workstation Sink Installation in Columbia, SC

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are popular in modern kitchen remodels.
