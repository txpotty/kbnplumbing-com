    Expansion Tanks With Tankless Water Heaters in Columbia, SC

    Tankless water heaters hold very little water, so thermal expansion is minimal in most homes. However, homes with recirculation loops, storage buffer tanks or certain closed system setups may still benefit from an expansion tank. We evaluate your system and only recommend a tank when it serves a real purpose. This keeps you from paying for parts you do not need.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Columbia

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Columbia, SC

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Columbia

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Columbia is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a new one. We expertly remove the old unit, dispose of it responsibly and install a new, correctly sized expansion tank. This timely replacement prevents costly damage to your water heater and plumbing system and restores proper pressure control.
