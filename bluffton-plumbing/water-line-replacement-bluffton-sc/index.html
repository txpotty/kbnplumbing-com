    PEX Water Line Replacement in Bluffton

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Bluffton, SC

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Bluffton, SC

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and unheated garages are the ones most likely to burst during a South Carolina cold snap, since many homes were built with little insulation around them. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Bluffton
