    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Bluffton

    The cost of well pump replacement in Bluffton, SC typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary. Factors influencing the price include the type of pump, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or wiring upgrades. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a careful assessment.

    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, strange noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional for inspection.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Most well pump replacements are completed in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to ensure compliance with local building and health codes. KBN Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.
