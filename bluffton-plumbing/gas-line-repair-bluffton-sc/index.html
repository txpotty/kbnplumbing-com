    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Bluffton

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Bluffton, SC

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Leak Detection in Bluffton

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Bluffton

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Bluffton

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Most South Carolina homes sit on crawlspaces or slabs rather than basements, which puts supply and drain lines either under the floor or under concrete. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Repair Costs in Bluffton, SC

    Simple connector or fitting repairs often cost $150 to $450. Interior section replacements tend to land between $400 and $1,200, and buried line replacements often run $1,000 to $4,000 or more.     Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Bluffton

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Bluffton

Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.
