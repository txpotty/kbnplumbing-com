    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Bluffton

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Bluffton

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Bluffton

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink.     Compression Faucet Repair in Bluffton

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Bluffton, SC
