    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Bluffton

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Bluffton

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Main Water Line Repair in Bluffton

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, and we dig one hole instead of a trench across your yard.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Bluffton, SC

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. Ours is a 24 hour line. KBN Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, which saves you from a weekend on a temporary patch. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. That happens more than you would think.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Bluffton, SC

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Bluffton

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Bluffton, SC

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Water pouring into a yard undermines everything around it. KBN Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Bluffton

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
