    PEX Pipe Replacement in Bluffton, SC

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Bluffton

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Bluffton, SC

    Even with softer supply water in Bluffton, a regular water heater flush still helps clear the sediment that settles in the bottom of the tank. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.
