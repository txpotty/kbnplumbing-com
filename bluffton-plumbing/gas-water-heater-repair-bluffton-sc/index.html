    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Bluffton

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Bluffton

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Bluffton, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. One goes in while we are there.

    Tank Leaks and Anode Rods in Bluffton

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Even with softer supply water in Bluffton, a regular water heater flush still helps clear the sediment that settles in the bottom of the tank. The tech pulls the anode, checks what metal remains and swaps it if bare core wire shows. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Bluffton home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. At that point the tank has to go.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Bluffton, SC

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. The burner comes out, the ports get brushed and the orifice gets cleared. We also confirm the orifice is drilled for the fuel you have, because a natural gas orifice on a propane heater, or the reverse, will soot up the flue and push carbon monoxide levels well past normal. You want a steady blue flame. Rust flakes on the burner tell us to look up the flue.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Bluffton, SC

    Atmospheric tanks rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue with an upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot. A loose, rusted or sagging section lets exhaust spill back into the house. The vent gets repaired. Each joint gets three screws, the connector gets proper clearance from wood and drywall, and we run the heater for several minutes with a smoke test at the draft hood to watch the exhaust go up. We do not leave with the heater spilling.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Service in Bluffton

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. We pull the pilot assembly and look at the thermocouple tip, the pilot orifice and the piezo igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Most trucks carry the common pilot kits.
