    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Bluffton

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Sewer Line Repair in Bluffton

    Sewer line damage underground can show up as foul odors, slow drains or soggy spots in your yard. We diagnose breaks, blockages or collapses in your main sewer line using HD camera inspections. Our repairs range from targeted patch repairs to full line replacement, often using trenchless methods to minimize disruption to your landscaping and concrete in Bluffton.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Bluffton, SC

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Bluffton, SC.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Bluffton, SC

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk. Full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too damaged. We explain each method and its cost so you can choose the best option.
