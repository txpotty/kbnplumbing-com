    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Bluffton, SC

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems.     Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Bluffton, SC

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Bluffton, SC

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast.
