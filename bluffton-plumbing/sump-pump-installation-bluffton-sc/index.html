    Sump Pump Replacement in Bluffton, SC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Bluffton, SC

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Bluffton, SC

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Bluffton, SC

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Bluffton

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Bluffton

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bluffton, SC

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Bluffton

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Bluffton

    Sump pump installation in Bluffton, SC runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The pump type and whether we dig a new pit move the price most. A battery backup raises the total. We quote it in writing after a site visit.

    The pump has its manufacturer warranty, and KBN Plumbing warranties the installation labor. We spell out the terms in your quote.

    Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty make up most of what we install. All four have good parts support. We pick by pit size and lift.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. A pump that cycles every few minutes through a wet spring wears out faster than one that runs a few times a month, and cheap switches fail first. We can check yours.

    Manufacturer warranties usually run three to five years, longer on premium models. Our labor carries its own warranty.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space.     Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. A flooded basement settles it.
