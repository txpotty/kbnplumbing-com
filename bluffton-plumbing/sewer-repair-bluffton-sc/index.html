    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Bluffton, SC

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can shift buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bluffton

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
