    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Myrtle Beach, SC

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Myrtle Beach

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Myrtle Beach. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. KBN Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Myrtle Beach, SC

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Myrtle Beach, SC, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Myrtle Beach

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Myrtle Beach, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines.
