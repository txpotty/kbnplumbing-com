    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Myrtle Beach

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Myrtle Beach, SC

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Most South Carolina homes sit on crawlspaces or slabs rather than basements, which puts supply and drain lines either under the floor or under concrete. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
