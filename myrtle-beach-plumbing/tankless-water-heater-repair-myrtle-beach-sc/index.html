    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers.     Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Myrtle Beach

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and it usually traces to scale or a backed-up condensate line.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, and one burned element means weak heat at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
