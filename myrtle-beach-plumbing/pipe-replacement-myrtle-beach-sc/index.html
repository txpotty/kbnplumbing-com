    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Myrtle Beach

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Myrtle Beach

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Before a forecast freeze, disconnecting garden hoses, covering hose bibs and letting a faucet drip is usually enough to protect a home. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.
