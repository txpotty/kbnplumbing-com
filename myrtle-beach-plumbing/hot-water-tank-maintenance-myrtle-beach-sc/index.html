    Electric Thermostat Service in Myrtle Beach

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. Attic tanks need a working pan.     Standard Tank Flushing in Myrtle Beach

Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.
