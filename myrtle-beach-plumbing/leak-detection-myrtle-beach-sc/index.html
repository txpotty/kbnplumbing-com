    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Myrtle Beach

    Most leak detection in Myrtle Beach, SC runs $250 to $800, based on how many lines need testing and where the leak sits. Slab and buried leaks land on the high end. You get a written estimate after the first assessment.

    Pipe material matters more than brand. Galvanized rusts out at threaded joints. Old polybutylene fails early, and even copper or PEX can leak from bad installation, high water pressure or a foundation that moved.

    A water bill that climbs, a damp ceiling spot or the sound of water when nothing is on tends to point to a hidden leak. So does visible mold. Do not wait on these, since they rarely fix themselves.

    Figure on one to three hours for most homes. Slab leaks or multiple leaks can take longer, since each one has to be confirmed before we mark it.

    Keep static pressure under 80 PSI, and add a pressure regulator at the main if the house does not already have one. Replace old supply lines. Homes with repeated pinholes may be better off with a partial or full repipe in PEX.

    Checking the meter and dye testing a toilet are easy DIY steps. Beyond that, it takes tools. A professional with acoustic sensors or tracer gas can pinpoint the leak without opening walls at random, which saves money on the repair.

    Shut every fixture off and watch the meter. If the dial or the little triangle leak indicator moves at all over ten minutes, water is escaping somewhere in the system. So is a sudden jump in your bill.
