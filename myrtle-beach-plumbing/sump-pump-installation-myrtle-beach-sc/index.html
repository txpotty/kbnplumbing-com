    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Myrtle Beach

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Myrtle Beach

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Myrtle Beach

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Myrtle Beach, SC

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.
