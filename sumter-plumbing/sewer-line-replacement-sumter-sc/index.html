    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Sumter

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This usually needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Sumter

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Sumter

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Sumter

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.
