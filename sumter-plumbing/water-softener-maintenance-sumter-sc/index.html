    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Sumter

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Sumter

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Sumter, SC

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Sumter

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Sumter, SC

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.
