    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Sumter, SC

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. KBN Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Sumter

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Sumter yard.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Sumter

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. Good prep makes a good liner.
