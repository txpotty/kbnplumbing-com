    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Sumter

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Sumter that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Sumter, SC

    Sewer lines in Sumter, SC can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells can stress buried sewer lines over time, leading to sags, separated joints and root intrusion. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Sectional Point Repair in Sumter

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
