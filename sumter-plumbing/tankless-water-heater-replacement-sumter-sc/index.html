    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Sumter

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Warm weather for most of the year means water heaters, irrigation lines and outdoor fixtures in South Carolina see year-round use with little seasonal rest. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Sumter

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are done in four to six hours.
