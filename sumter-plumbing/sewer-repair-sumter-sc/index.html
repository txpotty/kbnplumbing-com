    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Sumter

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.     Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Sumter

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Sumter, SC

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Sumter.
