    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Sumter, SC

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Most municipal water in South Carolina comes from rivers and reservoirs, so it tends to run fairly soft and leaves less scale than in many other states. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Sumter

    Gas tankless units in Sumter are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.
