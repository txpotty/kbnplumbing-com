    Reliable Shower Installation Services in Sumter, SC

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Sumter

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Sumter, SC

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are affordable and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or split.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Sumter

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Sumter, SC

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Walk-in Shower Installation in Sumter

    Walk-in showers offer accessibility and a modern look, and KBN Plumbing specializes in their installation. We handle the necessary drain relocation, waterproofing, and tile work to create a beautiful and functional walk-in shower. Proper slope for drainage is essential to prevent standing water, and we make sure this is done right. We also recommend appropriate grab bars and seating options.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Sumter, SC

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. KBN Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Sumter.

    Shower Pan Installation in Sumter

    A correctly installed shower pan is fundamental for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. KBN Plumbing expertly installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents expensive water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Sumter, SC

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.
