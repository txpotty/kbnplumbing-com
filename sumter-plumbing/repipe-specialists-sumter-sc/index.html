    PEX Pipe Replacement in Sumter, SC

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. KBN Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Sumter, SC, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Sumter

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Red clay dominates much of the upstate, while the coastal plain of South Carolina has sandy soil and a shallow water table that affects drainage and digging. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
