    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Sumter, SC

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Keep the salt about half full.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Sumter, SC

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Sumter

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Sumter

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Sumter puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Usually a stuck piston or a dead motor is to blame, and both can be replaced the same day.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Sumter

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Sumter, SC

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Sumter

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.
